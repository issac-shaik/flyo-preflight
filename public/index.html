        Load a demo ticket on the left. Preflight reads it, registers it with Flyo, checks the traveller's documents against entry, transit and Flyo rules, and chases anything missing over WhatsApp.

        1. Ticket in: PDF, photo or email text, read by a vision LLM or the built-in parser.
        2. Flyo subscribe right away, so missing info shows up as MISSING_INFO, not a rejection.
        3. Readiness: passport validity, visas, transit visas, name match, MRZ check digits, timing.
        4. Chase the traveller on WhatsApp, then send a full passenger update once they're ready to fly.
        5. Boarding pass out: webhook / status, delivered to WhatsApp.
